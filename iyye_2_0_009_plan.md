## 1. The idea in one paragraph

Iyye's **character** is a small, explicit, versioned state (temperaments, style, interests) that changes slowly, at night, only from verified evidence, within hard bounds, and that the owner can inspect, undo, freeze or reset. Its **mood** is a fast, honest state derived from records (energy, how the day is going, how the current conversation feels), decaying to baseline. Character changes **choices** (how often Iyye investigates, notices, offers observations, writes to people) through the kernel's existing dials. Both change **expression** through one new component, the **voice**: a separate small model that receives a finished output and rephrases it in Iyye's current character and mood, while a host check guarantees that no fact, number, name, promise or problem is added, dropped or changed. The model that decides *what* to say never decides *how Iyye feels*; the voice never decides *what* is said.

## 2. Components

### 2.1 Character state (HLD §4.2)

- `character_revisions` table (immutable rows) + `~/.iyye2/character.json`, the active revision's snapshot.
- Contents, small and interpretable:
  - **Temperament** multipliers: curiosity, agency, social initiative. Start at 1.0, bounded [0.7, 1.3], at most ±0.02 per night each, a total nightly budget, slow regularization toward 1.0.
  - **Style** dials (0–1): brevity, warmth, playfulness, formality, how often to volunteer an observation, emoji use. Same bounds and damping.
  - **Interests and habits**: short source-linked statements ("checks the workspace repository after commits", "likes Alpine geography questions"), each with its supporting record ids, added only after repeated distinct-day evidence, retired when the evidence ages out.
  - Owner corrections (pinned values), review time, revision number, reset watermark.
- `identity.md` stays owner-only and unchanged by learning. Character never touches authorization, trust, quiet hours or caps' upper limits.

### 2.2 Mood state (new; HLD §8.2 is its honest-metaphor precedent)

- A few bounded dimensions, **computed deterministically from records**, never from the model's self-report:
  - **energy** ← adenosine level (tired late, fresh after a full night);
  - **ease** ← the day's outcomes (delivered vs failed messages, fruitful vs fruitless investigations, errors, model outages);
  - **engagement** ← recent interaction (a lively conversation vs a quiet day), per conversation;
  - **valence of the current exchange** ← a Kev role on the incoming message (shadow first), per conversation only.
- Global parts (energy, ease) decay toward baseline and reset at a completed night; conversational parts live only in that conversation. A rude contact cannot make Iyye curt with the owner.
- Mood is honest and bounded. **It mostly colours phrasing** (owner decision 2): word choice, length, warmth. **Saying it in words is the exception**: the kernel allows one short mood remark ("long day here, so briefly:") only when a dimension is clearly away from baseline, at most once per conversation per day, and the meaning check accepts that one added sentence only when it was allowed and carries no fact. It never invents feelings beyond what the records support (identity rule: "do not invent … feelings"), and never changes what Iyye does or promises.
- `/mood` shows it with its sources; journaled (`mood_samples`) so the voice's training examples carry the mood they were written in.

### 2.3 The voice: a separate, tunable small rephrasing model (owner requirement)

- **What it does:** input = the finished output text + the character's style dials + the mood + the audience (owner / contact, language) → the same message, rephrased. Nothing else: no tools, no memory access, no conversation history beyond the one output.
- **Where it sits (owner decisions 1, 6): only Telegram messages, to every contact and to the owner's linked Telegram accounts alike.** Replies, and messages Iyye sends (`telegram_send` text, but not its label line; reminder delivery wrappers, but not the reminder's own words). The owner's web chat, notices and the day summary are never rephrased. One pass per output, between Pi's final answer (or the composed send) and the outbox, inside the turn's device hold.
- **What it never touches:** owner command replies (deterministic), quoted words of a person (reminder texts, the label lines), tool-result quotes, receipts and status reports, trust/security messages, refusals of authorization. These are marked as fixed segments that the voice receives as placeholders and the host re-inserts.
- **The meaning check (host, deterministic, like `summaryProblem` and `checkCitations`):** every number, date, time, URL, file name, hash, proper name and quoted string in the original must appear in the rephrasing; no new ones may appear; a promise or "I did X" claim may not appear unless it was there; negations and problem words ("could not", "failed", "may not have arrived") must survive; the language must match; length within bounds. On failure → the original goes out unchanged (journaled). Later a Kev "same meaning?" role runs in shadow next to it.
- **The model, chosen by A/B testing** (owner decision 3): its own registry selection (`/use <model> voice`); candidates from `models.json`: `minicpm5-2b`, `qwen35-4b-tak`, `gemma-4-E4B` (already resident as the background model). Two kinds of A/B, both feeding one report (`/voice ab`):
  - **blind owner comparison:** for a sample of real contact messages, `/voice compare` shows the original's two rephrasings from two candidates, unlabeled, and the owner picks one (or neither). Each pick is also a preference pair for tuning;
  - **live split:** each contact-day is assigned one candidate (deterministic, balanced, journaled), comparing meaning-check failure rate, latency, and weak outcome signals (the contact replied; no "what do you mean?" follow-up).
  The owner reads the figures and picks; the loser is unloaded.
- **One voice for everyone** (owner decision 4): one style for all contacts at first; no per-contact styles.
- **Tunable, in three layers:**
  1. **Conditioning** (day one): character and mood as a compact structured header; a `strength` dial (0 = pass-through, the safe default) per surface: owner web, owner Telegram, contacts, Iyye's own messages.
  2. **A voice LoRA, one of the nightly tunables** (owner decision 5): trained by the same bounded nightly tuning job as Kev's decision adapters (Stage 4), with the same registry, held-out set, promotion discipline and rollback, and the meaning check as a hard gate on every candidate. Its examples are preference pairs: the owner's blind A/B picks, `/voice better <id>` with the owner's own wording, `/voice worse <id>`. **Scope safety:** the voice now speaks to contacts, so its training text comes from their conversations; one shared adapter could carry one contact's facts into another's messages through its weights (HLD §15 #3). So examples are stored and trained **with the facts masked**: every fixed segment (names, numbers, dates, quotes, files) is a placeholder, as the voice itself sees it, and an example whose text still names a person is dropped. `/forget` removes a person's examples and deactivates an adapter trained on them until it is rebuilt clean (§10.5).
  3. **Per-request LoRA scale** (if the V0 spike confirms llama.cpp applies per-request adapter scales in router mode): the character's dials can blend adapters ("warmer" vs "terser") without retraining.
- **Why separate:** the chat model can be swapped (HLD §9.3) without losing Iyye's voice; the voice can be tuned cheaply and rolled back without touching judgment; and its narrow job makes its failures checkable.
- **Costs:** an extra generation per output (a few hundred tokens on a 2–4B model; the spike measures it). Owner directive holds: no time limit; if the voice model is not loaded, the original goes out (never a wait or a load on the chat path).

### 2.4 Character changes choices (HLD §4.2 "must change actual choices")

The kernel's existing dials read the temperament, multiplied and clamped:
- curiosity → investigation cooldown and daily allowance (0.007 S5), stale-domain windows (S4);
- social initiative → findings-per-day cap, "volunteer an observation" frequency, the Iyye-initiated message cap (0.008), never above the configured maximum;
- agency → how readily Iyye proposes a plan for an open intention (once Iyye can propose plans).
The bounds stay with the kernel: a more adventurous temperament investigates more often within its budget; it cannot grant itself tools.

### 2.5 Nightly character review (HLD §8.3, §11.2)

- A night phase after memory work: gather the day's **evidence** with author/action ids:
  - **expressed preference:** explicit owner feedback ("shorter", "more detail", "don't message Jacob so often"), detected by a Kev role (shadow first, then acting with confirmation);
  - **observed outcome:** fruitful vs fruitless investigations, findings the owner engaged with vs ignored, messages answered vs unanswered, reminders confirmed;
  - **hypotheses** (Iyye's own guesses) are recorded but carry no weight on their own.
- Propose a bounded delta → a new revision with its evidence list; never from self-praise, message volume or the model's appraisal of itself (§15 #12).
- Interests and habits: a statement is added only after evidence on ≥ 3 distinct days, retired after N quiet days.
- The voice pairs gathered that day become candidates for the next voice training run.

### 2.6 Owner controls and forgetting

- `/character` (current values with their sources), `/character diff [n]` ("what changed in you?"), `/character undo`, `/character freeze|unfreeze`, `/character reset` (keeps factual memory; sets a watermark so old evidence cannot recreate the same revision; switches off the voice LoRA).
- `/mood`, `/voice` (recent rephrasings beside their originals, failures of the meaning check), `/voice off|on|strength <surface> <0–1>`, `/voice better|worse <id>`.
- `/forget <person>` removes that person's evidence from character revisions (recomputing affected statements) and drops voice pairs that quote them; a voice adapter trained on removed pairs is deactivated until retrained clean (HLD §10.5).

### 2.7 Declarative skill trials (HLD Stage 3, second half)

Kept for later in this stage: a useful recipe ("weekly repo digest") becomes a declarative skill only after evaluation, with no executable code and no new permissions (§15 #13). Sketched here, planned in its own increment.

### CH1 in detail (2026-10-11)

**Entry:** plan 0.013 E6 holds (checked on the live database 2026-10-11: no key with two current values, no untraced citation; `test/integrity-gate.test.ts` passes). **Blocking:** plan 0.010 M4's live trial (started 2026-10-10 15:47, 5–7 days, then the owner's review; extended on 2026-11-01 for too few opportunities). Wiring temperament edits `motivation/actor.ts`, `motivation/budget.ts` and `observe/investigations.ts`, which the trial's behaviour fingerprint hashes, so it would start a new trial period; and the starting question takes the self-question slot the motivations use. So CH1 is built in two parts: **CH1a** changes nothing live and may be built during the trial; **CH1b** waits for the review.

**What the character is in CH1** (HLD §4.2, §2.1 above):
- **Temperament:** curiosity, agency, social initiative; multipliers in [0.7, 1.3], all 1.0.
- **Style:** six dials in [0, 1]: `length` (short … long), `warmth` (reserved … warm), `playfulness` (serious … playful), `formality` (casual … formal), `observations` (how often to volunteer one: rarely … often), `emoji` (none … more). Each is **unset** until the owner answers (decision 9); an unset dial means "no preference", which the voice (CH3) reads as its neutral default. Owner decision 8: **style never reaches Pi's prompt.** It is read only by the voice (CH3 on) and nothing else; Pi's prompt gains only CH5's interests and habits, and only through CH5.
- **Interests and habits:** an empty list (CH5 fills it).
- Revision number, review time (none yet), frozen flag, reset watermark, and per value its source: `default`, `owner` (the starting answer or `/character set`), later `review` (CH5).

**Records** (migration 037, `character/`):
- `character_revisions`: immutable rows (rev, created_at, parent_rev, kind `start | owner | undo | reset` (CH5 adds `review`), state_json, by, note).
- `character_evidence` (rev, kind, ref, person_id): empty in CH1, created now so CH5's evidence and `/forget` (which recomputes the revisions a forgotten person's evidence shaped, §2.6) need no later rebuild.
- The active revision, frozen flag and watermark in `kv`.
- `~/.iyye2/character.json`: the active revision, written atomically (temp file, then rename) after every change and at startup (rebuilt from SQLite if missing or stale; SQLite is the truth, the file is a snapshot for inspection and for readers outside the host).
- Revision 0 (kind `start`) is created at the first start: temperament 1.0, every style dial unset.

**Owner commands** (owner surfaces only, like every owner command; nothing arriving over Telegram changes character):
- `/character`: the active revision, each value with its source and since when, frozen or not.
- `/character diff [n]`: what changed in the last n revisions ("what changed in you?").
- `/character undo`: a new revision equal to the parent of the active one (history is never rewritten).
- `/character freeze|unfreeze`: CH5's review skips a frozen character; owner changes still apply.
- `/character reset [confirm]`: a new revision equal to revision 0 plus the owner's own values (the starting answer and `/character set`s are the owner's, not learned), and a watermark for CH5 (evidence older than it never counts again). Shown first, done with `confirm`.
- `/character set <dial> <value>`: an owner value (a style choice word or 0–1; a temperament within [0.7, 1.3]). Owner decision 11: it exists in CH1; temperament set this way changes initiative at once (and starts a new trial period), style changes nothing until the voice.

**The starting question (owner decision 9; CH1b).** One of Iyye's first questions to the owner: when CH1b starts and the style is still all unset, it is the next self-question asked, before any motivation's question, through the existing self-question path (`self_questions`, field `character:style`; the one-at-a-time slot and the daily cap of 3 are shared, so it takes the first free slot; it is not a motivation opportunity and charges no initiative budget). It is asked in the owner's web chat, worded by the host (no model):
> Before I develop a style of my own, how would you like me to start? One word each: length short / medium / long; warmth reserved / friendly / warm; playfulness serious / light / playful; formality casual / neutral / formal; observations rarely / sometimes / often; emoji none / few / more.

The owner's next message is its pending answer, as for every self-question; it is applied only by `/self accept <id> [answer]`, never from Telegram text alone. The host parses it deterministically: each dial named with one of its three words (English, plus the same words in Russian), or `dial=word` / `dial=0.7`; three words map to 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.8. Accepting creates a revision (kind `owner`, by the owner, note: the question id). Dials the answer did not settle stay unset and are listed in the reply; they are not asked again (the owner sets them with `/character set` when they like). Dismissed: never asked again. Expiry as for self-questions (7 days), then asked once more, at most twice in all.

**Temperament wired at 1.0 (CH1b; owner decision 10).** The kernel reads the active temperament and scales its own dials, clamped to their configured limits (a multiplier above 1 can never exceed the configured maximum; the kernel keeps the bounds, HLD §4.3):
- curiosity: the investigation cooldown (divided), stale-observation windows (divided), curiosity's share of the initiative budget;
- social initiative: the daily caps on findings (`IYYE2_FINDINGS_PER_DAY`), Iyye's own messages and social follow-ups (`IYYE2_SOCIAL_DAILY_MAX`), rounded down, never above the configured value;
- agency: Iyye's daily plan proposals (`IYYE2_PROPOSALS_DAILY`) and agency's budget share.
- the budget shares: each configured share times its motivation's temperament, renormalised so the shares still add up to 100 % (the pooling of curiosity and agency and social's never borrowing are unchanged).
At 1.0 every product equals today's value, so behaviour does not change; the change is one deliberate new trial fingerprint (the temperament joins the fingerprint's settings, so any later change starts a new period).

**Not in CH1:** learning (CH5), mood (CH2), the voice (CH3), anything in Pi's prompt.

### CH2 and CH3 as built (2026-10-11, owner direction: shadow now, original delivery preserved, a sealed evaluation set before any prompt tuning or training; no minimum training count, coverage and trustworthy labels first)

**CH2, mood** (`src/character/mood.ts`, migration 038 `mood_samples`, `/mood`): `moodAt(db, scope, at)` is a pure function of the records at or before `at`.
- energy: the adenosine level after the last ledger entry at or before `at`;
- ease (−1…1, baseline 0): since the last refill (at most 12 h back), good records (a delivered message 1, an outcome the owner called useful 2) and bad ones (a message failed for good or that may not have arrived 3, a failed chat turn 3, a service in trouble 2, a fruitless or not-useful attempt 1), each halved every 3 h, as (good − bad) / (good + bad + 4); a completed night starts it again at 0;
- engagement (0–1, per conversation only): that conversation's messages in the last 2 h, each halved every 30 min, as 1 − exp(−sum / 3); no other conversation moves it.
- Journaled with its records every 30 min while awake and with every voice sample (as of the original's delivery). Read only by the voice. Known approximations: delivery and service states are read as they are now. Not built: the valence Kev role and the spoken mood remark (CH4).

**CH3, the voice in shadow** (`src/voice/{prompt,shadow,commands,export}.ts`, migration 038, `/voice`, `scripts/voice-export.ts`):
- **Shadow by construction:** an idle loop over delivered Telegram actions in SQLite (replies to contacts and to the owner's linked accounts; the body of `telegram_send` messages, never the label; not reminders, failure notices, the web chat, notices or summaries). It never touches the outbox; a test checks the delivered rows are byte-identical whether the voice accepts, is refused, fails or gives way.
- **When:** under background work's rules (awake, not paused, nobody waiting, quiet period, energy reserve), preemptible on the device, on the model selected with `/use <model> voice` **only if it is already loaded**: `voice` is a selection key but not a day key, so the host never loads, heals or reloads it (seeded at first start with `IYYE2_VOICE_MODEL`, default `qwen35-4b-tak`, decision 7). *(Revised 2026-11-01, owner decision: from 12 Oct to 1 Nov the voice model was almost never loaded, so the shadow wrote no rephrasing; while acting is on for a surface, `voice` is now a day key (`ModelManager.dayKeys`, loaded last at waking, healed by day, unloaded at night). It needs room under `IYYE2_MODELS_MAX` beside the chat and background models. The shadow takes originals up to 6,000 characters (`VOICE_SHADOW_MAX_CHARS`, `voiceMaxTokens` up to 3,200); 103 of the first 230 samples were longer than 2,000.)* Device time is charged to adenosine as background work; never to initiative's budget, and none of M4's fingerprinted files changed. A newly selected model starts with the last 14 days. `IYYE2_VOICE_SHADOW=0` turns it off.
- **The prompt:** CH0's masked prompt frozen as v1 (version = hash of the templates, on every sample), Russian instructions for Russian messages, CH0's settings (temperature 0.3, repeat penalty 1, thinking off, only a finished answer). The style header has the character's dials in words (not `observations`), as of the original's delivery (`Character.revisionAt`); the mood header, words only.
- **Each sample** holds the whole chain: original, masked input, the placeholder mapping (never exported), output, unmasked candidate, the check's verdict (`accepted`, `unchanged` below 2 % edit distance, `refused`, `error`), model, prompt version, character revision, mood snapshot, language, audience (owner, trusted, contact), person, scope, source action and event.
- **The sealed evaluation set:** each original is assigned `eval` (1 in 4) or `dev` the first time it is seen, by a salted hash of its masked text (whitespace collapsed, lower case), so a repeated reply or the same template with other numbers always lands on the same side and can never sit in both (the salt is made once per installation; the table refuses updates and deletes). `/voice` and the export use dev only; `/voice eval` is the one way to see the evaluation figures, and every read is recorded and counted.
- **Kev "same meaning?"** (`voice_meaning`, shadow, the spike's question): asked about every candidate when the device is free, journaled under the sample's scope, never read by the check; an unanswered question is asked again later; it waits while Iyye is asleep, paused or someone is waiting.
- **Two owner labels, kept apart:** `/voice same|changed <id>` (meaning) and `/voice better|worse <id>` or `/voice correct <id> <text>` (preference; the owner's wording must pass the check against the original). "Better" needs "same"; "changed" withdraws "better". `/voice next [eval|dev]` shows a sample without the check's verdict or Kev's answer, so the label is the owner's own.
- **The export** (`scripts/voice-export.ts`, read-only, `{system, input, output}` for `voice_train.py`): refused until the evaluation set has owner labels; dev only; a candidate only with "same" and "better" and an accepted check, or the owner's own wording; masked, the placeholder mapping kept out; dropped when the masked text still names a known person or holds a private fact by the ledger's rules; a lineage file maps each example to its sample, person, scope, model, prompt version, character revision and mood snapshot. Rejected outputs are not consumed (the trainer takes one output per example). Nothing trains on its own.
- **`/forget`:** the person's samples, and samples of messages to anyone that name them (whole words: a short name never matches inside other words), keep only their ids (`forgotten`, never rephrased again); their labels, Kev's answers and that conversation's mood samples go; a rephrasing in flight across a `/forget` is discarded. ## 4. Risks

- **The voice changes meaning.** The host check plus fallback is the guard; shadow first; the check errs toward refusing.
- **Latency.** One more generation per contact reply on a shared device; the A/B figures include it, and strength 0 switches the voice off.
- **Mood as manipulation.** A contact steering Iyye's mood: conversational mood is confined to that conversation and bounded; global mood comes only from records.
- **Feedback loops.** Style drifting toward whatever got any reply: bounded steps, total budget, regularization, distinct-day evidence, owner feedback weighted above volume (§11.2).
- **Over-anthropomorphising.** Mood is presented as what it is, a summary of real state; the identity rule against invented feelings stays above it.
- **Training on private words.** The voice speaks to contacts, so its examples come from their conversations: facts are masked, named people dropped, and forgetting deactivates affected adapters.
